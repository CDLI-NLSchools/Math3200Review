Advanced options, question levels

Levels follow the course blueprint. The Mid-course preset uses the published mid-course blueprint mix: about 20% Level 1 (quick, one or two steps), 62% Level 2 (application: carry out the prescribed method), 18% Level 3 (higher-end thinking: analyse, justify, evaluate, transfer). The end-of-course blueprint has not been published yet, so the Final review preset uses 20 / 60 / 20, the combined proportions of the most recent published table, as an interim calibration.
